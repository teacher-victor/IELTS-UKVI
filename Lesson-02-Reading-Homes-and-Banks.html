Grammar

Describing places and rules

12 min

A · There is / there are

Adverts and notices describe what a place has. We use there is for one thing and there are for more than one.

One thing (singular)More than one (plural)
+There is a garden.There are two bathrooms.
–There isn't a lift.There aren't any shops nearby.
?Is there parking?Are there any buses at night?
!

Use any in negatives and questions with plurals: There aren't any pets. Are there any rooms?

Exercise 1

Choose the correct words.

  3. ______ a bus stop near the flat?
  4. Sorry, there ______ any rooms free this month.
  5. ______ any shops in the area?
  6. There ______ a washing machine, so I go to the launderette.

B · Can / can't for rules

Notices tell you what is allowed and not allowed. In IELTS, the text and the question often use different words for the same rule.

No smoking
You can't smoke.
No pets
You can't have animals.
Bike storage
You can keep a bike.
Permit holders only
You can park only with a permit.

Exam strategy

Skim, scan, and True / False / Not Given

8 min
Skim

Read fast for the general idea

Read the title, the headings and the first line of each paragraph. Ask: What is this text for? An advert? A guide? Rules? Spend 1 minute only.

Scan

Look for one piece of information

Choose a key word from the question — a number, a name, a day. Move your eyes quickly down the text until you find it. Then read that sentence carefully.

True, False or Not Given?

!

Not Given is not the same as False. If the text doesn't talk about it, the answer is NOT GIVEN — even if it is probably true in real life. Use only the text, not your own knowledge.

Exercise 5

Text B

Northgate Bank · New to the UK? Opening your first bank account

Moving to the UK for work? A UK bank account makes life much easier. Your employer will pay your salary into it, and you can pay your rent and bills automatically by direct debit.

What you need

You can apply in any of our branches or in the Northgate app. You need two documents: proof of identity, such as your passport, and proof of your UK address. Many newcomers do not have a gas or electricity bill yet. Don't worry — we also accept a tenancy agreement, or a letter from your employer on company paper dated in the last three months.

Your account

Our Basic Current Account has no monthly fee. You get a contactless debit card and access to online and mobile banking. The account does not include an overdraft, so you cannot spend more money than you have.

How long does it take?

If you apply in a branch with all your documents, we can usually open your account on the same day. Your debit card arrives by post within five working days, and your PIN comes in a separate letter a few days later.

Sending money home

You can send money to another country from the app. Fees depend on the country and the amount you send.

Text C

Harrow & Cole Engineering · Relocation guide for new international staff

Welcome to Harrow & Cole. This guide explains how we help you settle in Leeds during your first month with us.

Temporary accommodation

We provide a furnished company flat free of charge for your first four weeks. After that, you will need to find your own home. Our relocation partner, MoveWell, can arrange up to three viewings for you and explain your tenancy agreement.

Relocation allowance

You will receive a one-off payment of £1,500 with your first salary to help with costs such as a deposit or furniture. If you leave the company within twelve months, you must pay this money back.

Opening a bank account

You need a UK bank account before your first payday. HR can give you a letter confirming your address and your job, which most banks accept.

Right to work

Before you start, you will need to give us a share code from the UK government website so that we can check your right to work. On your first day, please also bring your passport.

Pay

Salaries are paid monthly, on the last working day of the month. You can see your payslip online.

Buddy scheme

Every new employee is given a 'buddy' — an experienced colleague from your team who can answer everyday questions about work and life in Leeds.

Teacher notes — lesson timing

00–05 Rafael's list + Task 1. Elicit what students know about UK banks.
05–17 There is / are (Ex 1). Notice signs and rules (Ex 2).
17–27 Banking cards, Ex 3. Statement reading (Ex 4): check DD vs SO.
27–35 Skim/scan, TFNG diagram, Ex 5–6. Stress: Not Given ≠ False.
35–55 Mock exam, 25 minutes (allow the whole time for A2 students).
55–60 Text Guide: show where the answers are; set homework.
!

Watch for: students choosing FALSE when the text is silent (Q10, Q13); choosing G "passport" in Q19 because the word appears in the text.

Homework (about 45 minutes)

  1. Text Guide: read the three texts again with the answers on. Note 5 pairs of "same meaning, different words". (15 min)
  2. Real UK life: open the "new customers" page of two UK banks. Write down what documents each one asks for. (10 min)
  3. Vocabulary: learn the 12 banking words. Say one sentence with each word out loud. (10 min)
  4. Speed: read one real room advert in 1 minute and say its rent, bills, rules and location. (10 min)
