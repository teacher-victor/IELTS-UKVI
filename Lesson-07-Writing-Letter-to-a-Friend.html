Exam strategy

Writing an informal letter

8 min

In GT Task 1 you may write to a friend. Examiners check that your tone is right: friendly and natural, but still clear and organised — with all three bullet points.

Formal (to a landlord or a company)Informal (to a friend)
Dear Mr Patel, / Dear Sir or Madam,Dear Ana, / Hi Ana,
I am writing to inform you that…Guess what? / I've got some great news!
I would be grateful if you could…Could you…? / Why don't you…?
I amI'm (contractions are fine)
I look forward to hearing from you.Write soon! / Can't wait to hear from you!
Yours sincerely, / Yours faithfully,Lots of love, / Best wishes, / See you soon,
!

Informal ≠ careless. Use paragraphs, full sentences and linking words. Don't use text-message spelling (u, gr8) or emojis in the exam.

IELTS General Training Writing · Task 1

Time: 20 minutes

  1. Read the task carefully. Plan for about 3 minutes.
  2. Write at least 150 words. Answer all three bullet points.
  3. Use a friendly, informal style — but full sentences and paragraphs.
  4. Choose how to write: on the answer sheet or on screen, like the computer test.
Writing Task 1

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

You have recently moved to a new town in the UK to start a new job.

Write a letter to a friend in your home country. In your letter

  • describe the town
  • tell your friend about your new job
  • invite your friend to come and visit you

Write at least 150 words.
You do NOT need to write any addresses.
Begin your letter as follows:
Dear ……………,

Plan (3 min):
Opening — a friendly first line + why you're writing
P1 — the town: 3 adjectives + 2 details
P2 — the job: what you do, people, how you feel
P3 — the invitation: when, what you can do together
Ending — Write soon! / Lots of love,

Letter A · about Band 5 · 151 words

Dear Ana,

How are you? I am fine. I write to you because I am live now in Leeds, in England.TA: The purpose is clear, but "I write to you because" sounds too formal for a friend.

Leeds is big city and is very nice.GRA: Missing articles (a big city) and a missing subject (it is). Errors like this are frequent. There are many shop and a big market. The weather is very cold and is raining every day, I don't like. The people is friendly.LR/GRA: "people are". Vocabulary is simple: nice, big, good are repeated. I have a room in a house with other people. The food is expensive but the market is more cheap.

My job is electrician in the hospital. I start two weeks ago.GRA: Past simple needed: "I started two weeks ago". The work is good but is difficult because the people speak very fast and I don't understand everything. My boss is nice. The hospital is very big and modern.

You can come to visit me. You can stay in my house and we can go to the city centre and the market.TA: The invitation is there, but with no time (when?) and little detail. "Can" is repeated three times. It will be very good.

I am waiting your answer.CC/TA: Ending and sign-off ("Best regards") are too formal for a close friend — the tone is not consistent.

Best regards,
Rafael

Letter B · about Band 7 · 181 words

Dear Ana,

Guess what? I've finally moved to Leeds! I'm sorry I haven't written for so long, but the last few weeks have been really busy.TA: A friendly, natural opening that makes the purpose clear. The tone stays informal to the end.

Leeds is a lively, multicultural cityLR: Precise adjectives from this lesson, used naturally. in the north of England. It's quite big, but I can walk almost everywhere. There's a huge market where you can buy food from all over the world, and the parks are really green — althoughCC: A range of linking words: although, but, so, and. it rains a lot, of course! I'm renting a room in a shared house near the university, and my housemates are very friendly.

My new job is going well. I'm working as an electrician at a hospital, so every day is different. My colleagues are helpful, and my boss gave me a buddy to answer all my questions.GRA: Present continuous, past simple and present perfect used accurately. The English is still hard sometimes, but I'm learning fast.

Why don't you come and visit me in the spring? You could stay in my room, and we could take the train to York for a day.TA: Bullet 3 — a clear invitation with a time and a plan. It only takes half an hour!

Write soon and tell me all your news.

Lots of love,
Rafa

Marking guide

How Task 1 is marked

The same four criteria as Lesson 3, each worth 25%. In an informal letter, tone is part of Task achievement: a letter to a friend that sounds like a business letter loses marks. See the official public band descriptors on ielts.org for the full wording.
