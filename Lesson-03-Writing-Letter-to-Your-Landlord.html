Grammar

Polite requests and linking words

12 min

A · Polite requests

In a letter to a landlord or a company, direct orders sound rude. Use these polite forms instead.

Too direct ✗Polite ✓ (good for IELTS letters)
Fix the heating.Could you fix the heating, please?
I want a new washing machine.I would like a new washing machine.
Send someone tomorrow.Would it be possible to send someone tomorrow?
Repair it quickly.I would be grateful if you could repair it as soon as possible.
Call me.Please contact me on 07946 352118.
!

After could you / would it be possible to / I would be grateful if you could, use the base verb: could you send — not could you sends or to send.

Life skill · Urgent or not urgent?

UK

Your landlord must repair the heating, hot water, gas, electricity, pipes, toilets and the structure of the building.

Smell gas? Leave the house, don't use switches, and call the National Gas Emergency number: 0800 111 999.

For other urgent problems (no heating in winter, water coming through the ceiling), phone your landlord. For problems that are not urgent, write — a letter or email is a record.

Exam strategy

How to write a Task 1 letter

8 min
Time

About 20 minutes

3 min to plan · 15 min to write · 2 min to check.

Length

At least 150 words

Under 150 words loses marks. Aim for 160–180.

Content

All 3 bullet points

Missing one bullet is the most common reason for a low score.

Format

Full sentences only

No bullet points or notes. You don't need addresses.

Formal, semi-formal or informal?

You are writing to…StartFinishStyle
someone whose name you don't know (a company, a manager)Dear Sir or Madam,Yours faithfully,Formal: no contractions (I am, not I'm)
someone whose name you know (your landlord, Mr Patel)Dear Mr Patel,Yours sincerely,Semi-formal: polite, clear
a friendDear Tom, / Hi Tom,Best wishes, / See you soon,Informal: contractions OK, friendly

Writing Task 1

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

You have just moved into a rented flat. There is a problem with the heating.

Write a letter to your landlord. In your letter

  • introduce yourself
  • describe the problem with the heating
  • say what you would like your landlord to do

Write at least 150 words.
You do NOT need to write any addresses.
Begin your letter as follows:
Dear Mr Patel,

Plan (3 min):
P1 — name, flat, when you moved in, why you are writing
P2 — what is wrong, since when, how it affects you
P3 — polite request + when you are at home + contact

Letter A · about Band 5 · 151 words

Dear Mr Patel,

I am Rafael, I am living in your flat in Hyde Street.TA: Bullet 1 is answered, but with very few details (which flat? when did he move in?). I am from Brazil and I am electrician in the hospital. I write because the heating is broken.

The heating is not working since one week.GRA: Wrong tense. Correct: "The heating has not worked for a week." The radiators is coldGRA: Plural subject → "are". Errors like this are frequent in the letter. and the flat is very cold. I have cold every night and I can't sleep good.LR/GRA: "sleep well". The word "cold" is repeated four times — the vocabulary is limited. I don't know what is the problem. There is a red light on the boiler and the flat is cold. The hot water is ok, but the shower is not very hot.

Please you fix the heating quickly because is very cold for me.TA: Bullet 3 is answered, but the request is too direct for a landlord. Better: "I would be grateful if you could…" I am work in the day, but I am in home in the evening and in the weekend. You can call me in my mobile, the number is 07946 352118. I wait your answer soon.CC: Paragraphs are clear, but linking is only "and / but". Ideas are not always connected.

Thank you very much for all your help.

Yours sincerely,
Rafael

Letter B · about Band 7 · 165 words

Dear Mr Patel,

My name is Rafael Costa, and I am the new tenant in Flat 3 at 14 Hyde Street. I moved in on 1 November,TA: Bullet 1 fully covered with useful details. The purpose of the letter is clear from the first paragraph. and I am writing to tell you about a problem with the heating.

Since last Monday, the heating has not been working properly.GRA: Accurate present perfect continuous with "since". A good range of structures with few errors. The radiators in the bedroom and the living room are completely cold, althoughCC: A range of linking words (although, so, but, alternatively) used naturally. the hot water is fine. I have checked the boiler, and there is a red light on it. The weather is getting colder, so the flat is now very uncomfortable, especially at night.LR: Precise, natural vocabulary: "properly", "completely cold", "uncomfortable", "arrange a time".

I would be grateful if you could send an engineer to repair the heating as soon as possible.TA: Bullet 3 — a polite, clear request with practical details (when he is at home, a key option, phone number). I am at work from 8 am to 5 pm on weekdays, but I am at home every evening and at the weekend. Alternatively, I can leave a key with my neighbour. Please contact me on 07946 352118 to arrange a time.

Thank you for your help.

Yours sincerely,
Rafael Costa

Compare

Marking guide

How Task 1 is marked

Examiners give a band (0–9) for each of four criteria; each is worth 25%. Task 2 counts twice as much as Task 1 in the final Writing score. Below is a simplified guide for bands 4–7 — see the official public band descriptors on ielts.org for the full wording.

BandTask achievementCoherence & cohesionVocabularyGrammar
7All bullets covered and well developed; clear purpose; the tone fits.Logical order; a range of linking words used well.Enough range to be precise; few word errors.A mix of simple and complex sentences; most sentences without errors.
6All bullets covered; some could be more developed; tone mostly fits.Clear overall; linking sometimes mechanical or faulty.Adequate range; some errors that don't block meaning.Some complex sentences, with errors that rarely cause problems.
5Bullets addressed but some are thin or unclear; tone may vary.Some organisation; limited or repetitive linking.Limited range; repetition; spelling errors noticeable.Mainly simple sentences; frequent errors.
4Not all bullets covered; purpose unclear.Ideas not well organised; little linking.Basic words, often repeated or wrong.Very limited structures; errors often cause confusion.

Teacher: mark the student's letter

Examiners give a whole band (0–9) for each criterion. Choose one for each; the Task 1 band is calculated.
