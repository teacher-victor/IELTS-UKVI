Life skill: Your rights as a customer in the UK

UK

Faulty goods: you usually have the right to a full refund within 30 days. After that, the shop can repair or replace it first.

Online or phone shopping: you usually have 14 days to change your mind and cancel, even if nothing is wrong.

In a shop, if you just change your mind: the shop doesn't have to give a refund — but many do. Read their returns policy and keep your receipt.

Text B

Harper's Department Store: Returns and refunds

Changed your mind?

Return unused items with the receipt within 28 days for a full refund. Without a receipt, we can offer an exchange or a gift card, but not a cash refund.

Sale items

Sale items can be returned within 14 days, but only for an exchange.

Faulty items

If something is faulty, you can return it within 30 days for a full refund, even without the original packaging. After 30 days, we will repair or replace the item.

Online orders

You have 14 days from delivery to cancel. Return your order by post, free of charge, using the label in the parcel, or take it to any of our stores.

Refunds

Refunds are paid to the original payment method within five working days.

Exceptions

For hygiene reasons, we cannot accept returns of earrings, underwear or opened cosmetics unless they are faulty.

Text C

Harrow & Cole Engineering: Company mobile phone policy

Who gets a phone?

All engineers who work on customer sites are given a company smartphone. Office staff can request one from their manager.

Personal use

You may use the phone for personal calls and messages, but please keep this reasonable. The company pays for up to 5GB of personal data each month; any extra will be taken from your salary.

Travelling abroad

Before you travel, contact the IT helpdesk at least 48 hours in advance so that they can turn on international roaming. Do not turn it on yourself.

Safety

Never use the phone while driving, even with a hands-free kit. You may only use it in an emergency, when the car is parked safely.

Loss or damage

If your phone is lost or stolen, report it to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours so that it can be blocked. You will not be charged for the first replacement.

Leaving the company

Return the phone and charger to HR on your last day. Please remove your personal photos before you return it.
